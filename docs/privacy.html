Privacy policy

Solace · Last updated: 18 November 2026

Solace is a wellness app. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and verified email address from Google. We never see your Google password. If you use email sign-in, your password is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Things you choose to add: mood check-ins, tags and notes, answers to the gentle check-ins, your goals, reminder settings, and your chat messages with Sunny.
  • What Sunny remembers: short facts taken from your chats (for example, "has exams coming up"), so conversations feel personal. You can see, delete or clear these at any time, or switch memory off.
  • Progress: activity you complete in the app, such as XP, streaks and badges.

Location

If you tap "Use my current location" on the therapists screen, the app asks for permission and uses your location on your device only, to fill in a place name for a map search. Your location is not sent to or stored on our server.

How your information is used

  • To run the app: sign you in, save your check-ins and chats, and show your progress.
  • To power Sunny and daily content. Your messages, and, if memory is on, relevant details such as recent moods and remembered facts, are sent from our server to Google's Gemini AI service to generate replies, the daily note and the daily challenge. They are used only to produce that response.
  • If a message suggests you may be in danger, the app replies with crisis helpline information before anything is sent to the AI service.
We do not sell your information, show ads, or use it to track you across other apps.

Where it is stored

Your account data and chats are stored on our server (hosted on Render). Reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. Your login session is kept in your phone's secure storage.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to You and tap "Delete my account and data". This permanently removes your account, chats, memories, check-ins and reminders.
  • Control memory: view, delete or clear what Sunny remembers, or turn memory off in the You tab.
  • Reminders: change or turn off any reminder in the You tab, or in your phone's notification settings.

Children

Solace is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Not medical advice

Solace offers self-care activities and friendly check-ins. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or replace professional care. If you are in crisis, contact a local helpline: in India, Tele-MANAS is free, 24 hours, at 14416; in an emergency call 112.

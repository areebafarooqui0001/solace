Solace

A calm place to land, one small thing at a time.

Solace is a wellness app for Android. It pairs simple daily habits with Sunny, an AI companion who understands how you feel, remembers what matters to you, and suggests small things that can help.

What you can do
  • Check in with your mood in one tap and see your week.
  • Talk with Sunny, and view, delete or switch off everything Sunny remembers.
  • Get a short daily note, a personal affirmation and one small daily challenge.
  • Try guided breathing, gentle check-ins and calming guides.
  • Set reminders that work even when the app is closed.

Signing in

You can sign in with your email or with your Google account. Google is used only to confirm who you are (your name and email address).

Please note

Solace is not a medical service and does not give diagnoses or treatment. If you are in crisis, please contact a local helpline. In India, Tele-MANAS is free and open 24 hours at 14416; in an emergency call 112.
